## What is PauseAI?

PauseAI is an international movement of volunteers. We campaign for a pause on
the development of the most powerful AI systems until we know how to build them
safely and under democratic control. PauseAI Freiburg is a local group of
[PauseAI Germany](https://www.pause-ai.de/).

## What exactly do you ask for?

We ask for an international treaty that temporarily stops the training of the
most powerful general AI systems. The pause must stay in place until effective
safety measures and democratic control exist. See the
[PauseAI proposal](https://pauseai.info/proposal) for details.

## Are you against AI or against technology?

No. Many AI applications are useful, for example in medicine. We only want to
pause the development of the _most powerful_ systems, whose risks nobody can
control today.

## Which risks do you see?

Advanced AI can be misused, for example for cyberattacks or to develop
biological weapons. It can concentrate power in the hands of a few companies or
states. And nobody knows yet how to reliably control systems that are smarter
than humans.

We are not alone with these concerns. In 2023, hundreds of AI researchers,
among them Geoffrey Hinton and Yoshua Bengio, signed this
[statement](https://safe.ai/work/statement-on-ai-risk): "Mitigating the risk of
extinction from AI should be a global priority alongside other societal-scale
risks such as pandemics and nuclear war."

## Is a pause realistic at all?

International treaties are hard, but possible. Examples are the Montreal
Protocol that protects the ozone layer and the treaties against the spread of
nuclear weapons. Training the largest AI models needs special chips that only a
few companies make. This makes it possible to verify a treaty.

## What about China and other countries?

This is why we ask for an _international_ treaty. A pause only works if the most
important countries take part, including the USA and China. No country benefits
when nobody can control the technology.

## I am not an AI expert. Can I still help?

simply come to one of our [events](/en/events/).
