You do not need any prior knowledge about AI to get involved. Everyone who
wants to work for a safe development of AI is welcome.

## How you can help

- **Come to a meetup.** You find our next [events](/en/events/) on this
  website. Just drop by.
- **Talk to others.** Tell friends and family about the risks of advanced AI.
- **Write to your representatives.** Politicians need to know that this topic
  matters to you.
- **Become part of PauseAI.** Find more ways to help at
  [PauseAI Germany](https://www.pause-ai.de/) and
  [PauseAI Global](https://pauseai.info/join).
