## Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., 88 Colin P Kelly Jr St,
San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). For each request, GitHub processes technical
data such as your IP address, the time and your browser to deliver the page
and to protect it against attacks. The legal basis is our legitimate interest
in a secure and stable website (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). GitHub is certified under
the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. For details, see the
[GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## No cookies, no tracking

We use no cookies and no analytics, and we load no content from other servers
(e.g. fonts). Your browser stores your choice of light or dark theme locally
(local storage). This information does not leave your device (§ 25 (2) no. 2
TDDDG).

## Links to other websites

When you follow a link to another website, its privacy policy applies.

## Contact by email

When you send us an email, we use your data only to answer your request
(Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR).

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure
(Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and
objection (Art. 21). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory
authority (Art. 77), e.g. the
[State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information
Baden-Württemberg](https://www.baden-wuerttemberg.datenschutz.de/).
