Laiba Rehman gave this speech on 16 September 2026 at the PauseAI UK emergency
protest outside 10 Downing Street. The protest followed AI researcher Jacob
Coxon's resignation from Anthropic and demanded that Prime Minister Andy
Burnham convene an international pause treaty summit.
